Why it has to be on-chain

Off-chain

A bank that learns of a petition usually freezes the account.

Account frozen

On-chain

Nobody freezes a borrower's stablecoin wallet. Repayments keep flowing to token holders.

Wallet open

So the stop has to live in the loan contract.

Freezing is bank practice, not law · Drawstop protects one lender's facility, it freezes nothing else
