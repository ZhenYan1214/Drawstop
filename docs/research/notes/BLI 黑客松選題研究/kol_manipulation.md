# Crypto KOL / Finfluencer Manipulation, Undisclosed Paid Promotion, and Pump-and-Dump: Harm, Law, Enforcement, Tooling (research as of 2026-10-14)

Scope note: Most numbers below come from secondary press coverage of primary reports. Where I could open the primary source (FCA, ASIC, Solidus, The Block), that is noted. Facts from before 2024 are marked [OLDER].

## 1. Scale of harm: pump-and-dumps, memecoin rugs, KOL-promoted token losses (2024–2026)

### Takeaway
The harm is large and well measured at the token level. On pump.fun, roughly 98–99% of launched tokens behave like pump-and-dumps or rugs. Celebrity and political memecoins (TRUMP, LIBRA, HAWK) each produced hundreds of thousands of losing wallets, while a small number of insider wallets took most of the profit. The one peer-reviewed study of KOLs specifically finds that influencer tweets come before negative returns. There is little 2024–2026 academic work that ties named KOLs to losses, and that gap is the opening.

### Cited Findings
**Aggregate / platform-level**
- Chainalysis 2025 Crypto Crime Report (covers 2024): more than 2 million tokens launched in 2024; about 42% (~870,000) listed on a DEX; only 1.7% actively traded in the prior 30 days; more than 74,000 tokens linked to suspected pump-and-dumps in 2024, up from more than 42,000 in 2023 (about +75%), steady at about 3.5% of launches. — [Benzinga summary of Chainalysis](https://benzinga.com/z/30944828); [it-online](https://it-online.co.za/?p=298624) (secondary; I did not open the primary Chainalysis page)
- [OLDER] Chainalysis 2024 report (2023 data): 54% of Ethereum ERC-20 tokens listed on DEXs showed patterns suggestive of pump-and-dumps, but they made up only 1.3% of DEX volume. Operators made $241.6M in profit, and one wallet ran 81 schemes for $830K. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/215205/54-of-tokens-appear-to-be-pump-and-dumps-but-theres-good-news)
- [OLDER] Chainalysis (2022 data): of 40,521 tokens that gained traction, 9,902 (24%) fell 90% within a week. 445 actors accounted for 24% of these tokens, and creators made about $30M. — [Infosecurity Magazine](https://infosecurity-magazine.com/news/quarter-crypto-tokens-linked-pump); [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/chainalysis-rug-pull-report)
- I found no 2026 Chainalysis memecoin or pump-and-dump figure for 2025 activity (see Gaps). — [Chainalysis 2026 report landing](https://www.chainalysis.com/?p=26441)
- Solidus Labs 2025 Rug Pull Report (vendor research, primary page opened): 98.6% of pump.fun tokens "collapse into worthless pump-and-dump schemes" shortly after launch. The sample is more than 7 million tokens with at least 5 trades, from Jan 2024 to Mar 2025, and only about 97,000 kept more than $1,000 of liquidity. About 93% of 388,000 Raydium V4 pools showed soft-rug characteristics, with a median loss of about $2,832 and a maximum of $1.9M. The report has no KOL statistics; it lists "KOL tracking" only as an off-chain signal in its own Token Sniffer product. — [Solidus Labs](https://www.soliduslabs.com/reports/solana-rug-pulls-pump-dumps-crypto-compliance)
- Academic: Mancino (arXiv 2512.11850) on pump.fun in Q4 2024 finds that fewer than 2% of tokens graduated to major DEXs. — [arXiv 2512.11850](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.11850v2)
- Academic: "Coordinated Sniper Cohorts on Pump.fun" (arXiv 2607.02795, 2026) detects 1,012 persistent wallet rings (median size 2) at the top of first-buyer queues and publishes a catalogue and detection script. The authors do not claim the behavior is manipulation. — [arXiv 2607.02795](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.02795)
- Other pump.fun papers: Szwajcok et al., "Meme coin factories: Uncovering large-scale manipulations on pump.fun" (arXiv 2609.10246, title only seen); Marino et al., success prediction (arXiv 2602.14860); a cross-pipeline coverage paper (arXiv 2609.18975). — [arXiv 2609.18975](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.18975); [emergentmind topic page](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/pump-fun)
- The pump.fun class action alleges the platform extracted more than $5.5B from users (an allegation, not a finding). — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2025-07-23-burwick-law-names-solana-and-jito-executives-in-expanded-rico-case-against-pump-363996)

**KOL-specific evidence**
- Merkley, Pacelli, Piorkowski & Williams, "Crypto-influencers," *Review of Accounting Studies* 29(3), 2024 [data from 2020–2022]: about 36,000 tweets from 180 prominent crypto influencers covering more than 1,600 assets. Returns are positive at first, then −1.02% from day 2 to day 5, −2.24% at 10 days, −6.53% at 30 days, and about −19% at 3 months. The effect is strongest for self-described "experts" with large followings and for small caps. A buyer of small tokens on the tweet date who held for 30 days lost 7.9% (−62.8% annualized). The authors say this is consistent with pump-and-dump but "not a smoking gun." — [Kelley School blog](https://blog.kelley.iu.edu/?p=4898); [HBS Working Knowledge](https://hbswk.hbs.edu/item/when-celebrity-crypto-influencers-rake-in-cash-investors-lose-big); [RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/spr/reaccs/v29y2024i3d10.1007_s11142-024-09838-4.html)
- Coinwire industry analysis (not peer reviewed): more than 1,500 memecoins promoted by 377 X influencers (each with at least ~10K followers). More than 76% of the influencers had promoted memecoins that are now defunct, only 1% of promoted coins reached 10x, and accounts with more than 200K followers performed worst. Coverage is inconsistent ("76%" vs "two-thirds"). — [Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/coinwire-report-on-memecoin-influencers/)
- arXiv 2603.12000 (2026) frames crypto KOLs as a distinct influencer class whose posts on low-cap tokens can move prices and expose followers to pump-and-dumps. It is a background framing paper. — [arXiv 2603.12000](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.12000)
- "KOL rounds": KOLs get discounted token allocations and earlier unlocks than other early investors, sometimes at launch, which builds in an incentive to sell into follower demand. — [Bloomberg-syndicated coverage via itiger](https://www.itiger.com/hans/news/2518404972)

**Headline incidents (2024–2025)**

Nansen and Bubblemaps tie about $107M of liquidity pulled shortly after launch to insiders. Bubblemaps found 82% of supply in a single cluster. Hayden Davis told Coffeezilla he held more than $110M. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/libra-investors-lost-251-million-memecoin-javier-milei); [KuCoin on Bubblemaps](https://www.kucoin.com/learn/web3/what-is-bubblemaps-bmt-and-how-does-it-work)
- **$HAWK (Dec 2024, Haliey "Hawk Tuah" Welch)**: reached a $490M market cap, then fell about 93% within minutes. — [Winston & Strawn / Decrypt coverage](https://decrypt.co/312190/hawk-tuah-girl-sec-dropping-probe-into-meme-coin-tmz)
- **ATLAS (Dec 2025)**: Bubblemaps flagged 68 wallets holding 47% of supply as suspected insiders, alongside KOL promotion. — [MEXC news (secondary)](https://www.mexc.com/news/361960); [Bubblemaps blog](https://blog.bubblemaps.io/how-to-analyze-meme-coin-holders-with-bubblemaps/)
- Taiwan context: the FSC took down 34,679 fraudulent investment ads in 2025 and 50,549 in 2024. — [uanalyze](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/5426749667)

### Inferences
- Token-level base rates (98% of pump.fun tokens fail) are well established. What is missing is attribution: which KOLs promoted which tokens, whether they were paid, and whether they sold. A KOL track-record and disclosure layer would fill a real evidentiary gap.
- The repeated pattern in TRUMP, LIBRA and ATLAS (a handful of wallets profit, hundreds of thousands lose, and the evidence comes from post timestamps vs. on-chain trades) is exactly the evidence a "KOL narrative agent" could preserve.
- The Merkley et al. method (event study of returns after a tweet) can be reproduced as a per-KOL scoring metric.

### Gaps
- No primary Chainalysis 2026 figure for 2025 pump-and-dump or memecoin losses was found.
- No rigorous 2024–2026 dollar estimate of losses attributable specifically to KOL promotion. The 76% Coinwire figure is unverified industry data.
- Telegram pump-group studies (e.g., La Morgia et al., Xu & Livshits) are pre-2024 and were not re-verified this session.
- I could not open the findings of "Meme coin factories" (arXiv 2609.10246).

## 2. Law: US, UK, EU, Taiwan, Singapore, Australia, Hong Kong

### Takeaway
Two legal hooks recur across jurisdictions. The first is undisclosed compensation: US §17(b), FTC disclosure, UK s.21 FSMA and MAS ad rules. The second is promoting while trading against followers ("scalping"): explicitly named as manipulation in EU MiCA Art. 91, and fraud in the US. The big US shift came in Feb 2025, when SEC staff said typical memecoins are not securities. That weakens §17(b) for memecoins and pushes liability toward FTC, state law, CFTC/DOJ fraud and private class actions. The EU (MiCA, fully applicable since Dec 30, 2024) is now the clearest regime against KOL scalping.

### Inferences
- A legal-tech tool can encode a portable compliance rubric that works across jurisdictions: (a) was compensation disclosed (US FTC/§17(b), UK s.21, MAS, MiCA marketing rules)? (b) did the promoter hold or trade a position around the post (MiCA Art. 91 scalping, US fraud)? (c) is the token or platform licensed or approved (UK, Taiwan VASP Act)? Each is checkable from posts plus on-chain data.
- Because US securities law has pulled back from memecoins, private plaintiffs (class actions) and the EU/UK are now the main consumers of KOL evidence.
- Taiwan's new VASP Act gives a manipulation hook but no explicit KOL disclosure duty yet. The FSC's subsidiary rules (due by around Q1 2027) are a live policy window, and a tool could feed into that consultation.
- From background knowledge, not verified this session: MiCA Art. 7 (and Arts. 29/53) require crypto marketing communications to be clearly identifiable as marketing, fair, clear, not misleading and consistent with the white paper. Confirm on EUR-Lex before relying on this.

### Gaps
- Exact MiCA Art. 91 paragraph number and wording were not verified against EUR-Lex.
- No national-authority (NCA) MiCA Art. 91 enforcement against a crypto influencer was found for 2025–2026.
- Final text of the Taiwan VASP Act's unfair-trading articles (whether they cover spreading rumors or false information and scalping) was not retrieved.
- No FCA crypto-specific finfluencer prosecution was found; the cases found involve forex/CFDs.
- Australia: no ASIC crypto-specific court case was found.

## 3. Enforcement cases 2024–2026 and the evidence relied on

### Takeaway
Recent cases and investigations rely on the same evidence package: (1) timestamped social posts, (2) on-chain wallet clustering showing the promoter or insiders bought before and sold into the pump, (3) payment receipts to KOL wallets, and (4) supply concentration and liquidity pulls. Outcomes are mixed. Courts have dismissed weak influencer claims (Logan Paul) and unfrozen assets (LIBRA). Most of the evidence comes from private sleuths (ZachXBT, Bubblemaps, Nansen), not regulators.

### Cited Findings
- **ZachXBT price sheet (Sept 1, 2025, The Block, primary page opened)**: a spreadsheet of more than 200 influencers contacted for a token campaign, with prices and Solana wallet addresses plus links to on-chain payment receipts. About 160 accepted, and fewer than 5 labeled their posts as ads. Per-post prices ran from hundreds to five figures. The project was not named. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/368956)
  - Other reports: five tiers at $50–$60,000 per post (Cybernews), or $1,500–$60,000 per post with more than $1M total (ChainCatcher/TechFlow). — [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/crypto/hundreds-crypto-influencers-caught-deceiving-followers/); [ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2202681); [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/27945)
  - Follow-up on-chain analysis by @dethective alleged that KOLs traded the tokens they were paid to promote, buying just before and selling after posts (e.g., @0xSweep). These are allegations only. — [ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2202681)
- **LIBRA class action (Burwick Law)**: filed Mar 17, 2025 in NY Supreme Court against Kelsier Ventures (Hayden Davis), KIP Protocol and Meteora, then removed to SDNY (Judge Rochon). The complaint alleges one-sided liquidity with about 85% of supply withheld. Evidence includes the Jan 30, 2025 Casa Rosada meeting between Milei and Davis two weeks before the post, and Nansen/Bubblemaps on-chain flows. $57.6M USDC was frozen in June 2025 and later unfrozen. Argentine prosecutors sought to freeze $110M. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/burwick-law-file-class-action-libra-memecoin-scandal); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/336060/libra-prom?amp=1); [FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/argentine-prosecutor-moves-to-freeze-110m-tied-to-libra-memecoin/); [intellinews](https://www.intellinews.com/argentina-s-milei-faces-legal-pressure-in-us-over-libra-scandal-393878/)
- **Pump.fun RICO (Aguilar et al., SDNY 1:25-cv-00880)**: filed Jan 2025 and amended Jul 23, 2025, adding Solana Labs, Solana Foundation and Jito executives. Theories include illegal gambling ("slot machine"), wire fraud, unlicensed money transmission and securities claims. Jito parties were later voluntarily dismissed, and motions to dismiss were partly granted and partly denied (details not retrieved). — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2025-07-23-burwick-law-names-solana-and-jito-executives-in-expanded-rico-case-against-pump-363996); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/331528/solana-pump-fun-amended-rico-suit-alleging-meme-coin-gambling); [CourtListener filing](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.nysd.635992/gov.uscourts.nysd.635992.184.0.pdf)

### Inferences
- The decisive evidence is a time-aligned join of post timestamps, wallet trades and payment receipts. Today it is assembled by hand by sleuths after the collapse. Preserving it as it happens, in a hash-anchored and reproducible way, directly serves plaintiffs and regulators.
- The Logan Paul dismissal shows that vague hype is "puffery." Claims survive when there is concrete enrichment (selling into the pump, undisclosed pay) or specific false statements, which is what an agent should extract and score.
- The Gotbit sentencing ("losses could not be reliably calculated") shows that quantifying victim harm is a real gap. Per-wallet buyer loss computation after a KOL post is valuable.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 US criminal case against a crypto KOL specifically for scalping memecoins was found. McAfee (2021) remains the template.
- Holdings in the SDNY pump.fun motion-to-dismiss decision were not retrieved.
- No public court filing was found that admitted ZachXBT-style KOL payment spreadsheets as evidence.

## 4. Existing tools: what they do and do not do

### Takeaway
Existing tools fall into four buckets: attention/mindshare (Kaito, Cookie, LunarCrush, Santiment), KOL wallet PnL trackers (KOLScan, Solana Tracker, Arkham KOL labels), holder-cluster forensics (Bubblemaps, Nansen, Arkham) and crowd fact-checking (Community Notes). None of them combines post-level extraction, paid-promotion disclosure checks, the promoter's own on-chain trades and a tamper-evident evidence record. Several (Kaito "yaps," Cookie Snaps, KOLScan, now owned by pump.fun) are paid by, or aligned with, the attention economy they measure.

### Cited Findings
- **Kaito**: a "mindshare" metric (share of attention for a token or keyword over a window) plus "Yaps," which reward users for crypto content and affect airdrop eligibility. Its algorithm is criticized as an opaque gatekeeper. Pro analytics reportedly cost about $1,000/month. — [Spartan Group](https://www.spartangroup.io/insights/kaito-an-emerging-infofi-ecosystem); [CoinEx Research](https://www.coinex.com/en/insight/report/repricing-influence-in-web3-ii-the-financialization-of-information-685511241c6ecd062f24ab3a); [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/cookie-snaps-creator-network-decentralize-infofi/)
- **Cookie (Cookie3/Cookie DAO/Snaps)**: mindshare dashboards for 24h and 7d plus "signal scores" that filter out bots, farmers and low-credibility accounts. It publishes its scoring rules and offers free analytics. It also runs a paid creator network for projects. — [Cookie3 newsletter](https://cookie3.beehiiv.com/p/a-live-dashboard-in-under-5min-ad8a); [Cookie3 "gonna hire a kol"](https://cookie3.beehiiv.com/p/gonna-hire-a-kol); [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/cookie-snaps-creator-network-decentralize-infofi/)
- **KOLScan / Solana Tracker KOL API**: wallets mapped to public identities (name, X handle), with a live buy/sell feed and leaderboards by PnL, ROI, win rate and trade count. KOLScan is free and was reportedly acquired by pump.fun. — [Solana Tracker docs](https://docs.solanatracker.io/guides/kol-tracking); [Solana Tracker leaderboards](https://docs.solanatracker.io/guides/pnl-v2/leaderboards); [MadeOnSol comparison (possibly promotional)](https://madeonsol.com/compare/kolscan-vs-the-tie)
- **Arkham**: a KOL entity label (2025) for influencers with more than 100K X followers, so users can check whether KOLs hold what they promote. — [Coinpaprika](https://coinpaprika.com/news/arkham-s-new-tool-exposes-crypto-influencers-wallets/); [itiger](https://www.itiger.com/hans/news/2518404972)
- **Bubblemaps**: holder bubble maps that color wallets likely controlled by the same entity. Its revamp added "bundle" flags (same-block buys at launch), "cluster" flags (shared funding) and a per-token score. It was used in the LIBRA (82% in one cluster) and ATLAS investigations. — [Bubblemaps blog](https://blog.bubblemaps.io/how-to-use-bubblemaps-a-step-by-step-guide-for-beginners/); [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/learn/web3/what-is-bubblemaps-bmt-and-how-does-it-work)
- **Solidus Labs Token Sniffer** (compliance vendor) lists KOL tracking and X/Telegram/Discord/Reddit sentiment as off-chain signals for exchange listing and compliance teams. — [Solidus Labs](https://www.soliduslabs.com/reports/solana-rug-pulls-pump-dumps-crypto-compliance)
- **ZachXBT / Coffeezilla / @dethective**: manual investigations that combine leaked payment sheets with on-chain trades. This is the de facto accountability layer today. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/368956); [ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2202681)
- **X Community Notes**: the median time to a first note is about 10.4 hours, more than 90% of notes are never published, and effectiveness drops sharply with delay. I found no study on notes for undisclosed crypto paid posts. Many memecoin scam posts come from hijacked accounts (Nasdaq, Litecoin, the Robinhood CEO), which notes handle poorly. — [arXiv 2510.12559](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.12559); [Malay Mail on 90% unpublished](https://www.malaymail.com/news/tech-gadgets/2025/07/10/stuck-in-limbo-over-90pc-of-xs-community-notes-fact-checks-never-reach-the-public/183435); [arXiv 2409.08781](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.08781); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/memecoin-scam-promoted-through-compromised-x-accounts/)
- Mindshare and engagement metrics are inflated by bots and farmers. One proposed fix is weighting engagement by on-chain wealth. — [Mirana](https://mirana.xyz/research/how-projects-can-leverage-kaito-for-growth-and-influence); [0xkepler](https://0xkepler.substack.com/p/wtf-is-mindshare)

### Inferences (gap analysis)
- **No disclosure-compliance checking**: no tool found flags whether a KOL post carries #ad or a disclosure, or whether the KOL received tokens or stablecoins from the project treasury. ZachXBT found that fewer than 5 of 160 disclosed, which shows the gap is real and measurable.
- **No tamper-evident evidence trail**: tools show live dashboards. None hashes and timestamps posts, which can be deleted, together with linked wallet state for later litigation or regulatory use.
- **No accountability-oriented track record**: KOLScan ranks KOLs by their own PnL (who profits). The investor-protection metric would be follower outcomes after a call, such as Merkley-style post-call returns, the share of calls where the KOL sold within X hours, and dump-after-shill rate.
- **Conflicts of interest**: Kaito and Cookie monetize KOL attention, and KOLScan belongs to pump.fun, so a neutral regtech scorer has positioning room.
- **Narrative convergence across many KOLs** (the RYO-CHAN spotlight idea) maps directly onto coordinated-campaign detection: about 160 KOLs paid for the same token at the same time is exactly a "convergence" signal. The same agent can be framed as alpha (trade) or as a red flag (paid shill wave), and both are useful.

### Gaps
- I did not verify LunarCrush or Santiment features this session (they are generally social-volume and sentiment tools; confirm).
- CryptoRank KOL pages and other "shill detector" bots were not examined.
- No independent accuracy benchmarks exist for any of these tools.

## 5. Who would pay for or benefit from it

### Takeaway
The strongest near-term buyers are those with legal duties or litigation upside: exchanges and PPAETs under MiCA Art. 92 surveillance duties and listing due diligence, plaintiff firms (Burwick-style memecoin class actions), and regulators running finfluencer sweeps (17 regulators in 2026). Projects running KOL campaigns need disclosure-compliance proof. Retail benefits most but pays least.

### Cited Findings
- Exchanges and brokers (PPAETs) must run market-abuse detection under MiCA Art. 92. ESMA guidelines flag social media as a manipulation vector. — [Goodwin](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2025/05/insights-finance-ftec-fighting-market-abuse-in-crypto-assets); [ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-07/ESMA75-453128700-1039_Guidelines_on_supervisory_practices_to_prevent_and_detect_market_abuse__MiCA_.pdf)
- Compliance vendors already sell KOL and social signals to listing and compliance teams (Solidus Token Sniffer). — [Solidus Labs](https://www.soliduslabs.com/reports/solana-rug-pulls-pump-dumps-crypto-compliance)
- Plaintiff firms are active: Burwick Law (LIBRA, pump.fun, $M3M3 on Meteora, about $69M alleged) and Wolf Popper (pump.fun). — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/burwick-law-file-class-action-libra-memecoin-scandal); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2025-07-23-burwick-law-names-solana-and-jito-executives-in-expanded-rico-case-against-pump-363996); [Solidus](https://www.soliduslabs.com/reports/solana-rug-pulls-pump-dumps-crypto-compliance)
- Regulators run coordinated finfluencer sweeps (9 in 2025, 17 in 2026) and issue mass takedowns (more than 650 requests in 2025; 1,702 UK crypto alerts in the regime's first year). MAS and ASIC publish template letters and red flags, so they need scalable monitoring. — [FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/first-court-appearance-three-finfluencers-charged-fca-led-global-crackdown-illegal-promotions); [ASIC](https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-081mr-asic-continues-finfluencer-crackdown-alongside-global-regulators/); [Regulation Tomorrow](https://www.regulationtomorrow.com/2024/10/financial-promotions-quarterly-data-2024-q3/)
- AFS licensees in Australia must supervise the finfluencers operating under their licences; ASIC is reviewing licensees covering 15 finfluencers. This is a compliance-monitoring need. — [ASIC](https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-081mr-asic-continues-finfluencer-crackdown-alongside-global-regulators/)
- Taiwan fund houses must contractually supervise finance-KOL partners under SITCA self-regulation. — [Liberty Times](https://ec.ltn.com.tw/article/paper/1636342)
- Projects and agencies pay KOLs at scale ($50–$60K per post; more than $1M in one campaign), and FTC rules make advertisers and intermediaries liable for non-disclosure. — [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/crypto/hundreds-crypto-influencers-caught-deceiving-followers/); [Steptoe](https://www.steptoe.com/en/news-publications/ftc-unveils-new-endorsement-guides-and-proposed-rule-on-consumer-reviews-what-your-business-needs-to-know-and-opportunities-to-shape-whats-next.html)

### Inferences
- For the hackathon pitch, the best "legal tech" framing is "evidence-grade KOL call ledger." Each KOL call is recorded with hashed post content, timestamp, extracted token, sentiment, conviction and urgency, the KOL wallet's position before and after, disclosure present or absent, and follower-outcome returns. The same record serves the RYO-CHAN bounty (practice trades with a reasoning trail) and legal users (disclosure audits, scalping evidence, damages estimates).
- Retail could get a free "should I trust this call" score, while B2B buyers (exchanges, law firms) pay for exports and evidence packages.

### Gaps
- No market-sizing data was found on spend for crypto social-surveillance or compliance tooling.
- No interviews or evidence were found showing plaintiff firms buying such data, beyond their use of Nansen and Bubblemaps outputs.

## 6. Technical feasibility: data access in 2026

### Takeaway
The X API is now pay-per-use, about $0.005 per post read. Monitoring 20 KOL timelines is cheap: at about 20 posts per day each, that is roughly 12,000 reads per month, or about $60. Apify scrapers are cheaper still ($0.25–0.40 per 1,000 tweets) but fragile and against the ToS. Telegram added an explicit anti-scraping clause in Feb 2026, so use the official Telegram API (MTProto client) for public channels. On-chain data (Solana) is freely accessible via RPC and indexers.

### Cited Findings
- On Feb 6, 2026, X made pay-per-use the default and ended the free tier for new developers. Credits are prepaid in the Developer Console. Reads cost $0.005 per post returned and own-data reads $0.001. Posting costs $0.015 (plain) or $0.20 for a post with a URL (since Apr 2026). — [Open Terms Archive](https://opentermsarchive.org/en/memos/x-replaces-its-dollar200-entry-level-api-plan-with-a-pay-per-use-model/); [Gigazine](https://www.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260209-x-api-pay-per-use/); [opentweet.io](https://opentweet.io/x-api-pricing)
- One source reports a 2M post-read monthly cap before Enterprise is required (unverified). Legacy Basic ($200) and Pro ($5,000) are closed to new signups; Enterprise is reportedly $42K+/month. Since Oct 2, 2026, new accounts can earn one-time credits ($20 for a card, plus a match of up to $50 on the first auto-recharge), still rolling out. — [bundle.social](https://bundle.social/blog/x-api-pricing-2026-costs-limits); [zernio](https://zernio.com/blog/twitter-api-pricing); [opentweet.io](https://opentweet.io/x-api-pricing)
- Apify X scrapers cost about $0.25–$0.40 per 1,000 tweets (e.g., apidojo tweet-scraper V2, with a minimum of 50 per query). Platform plans run $0 (with $5 of usage) up to $999. They are good for historical pulls but weak at real time, and they break when X changes its front end. — [use-apify](https://use-apify.com/docs/best-apify-actors/best-twitter-scrapers); [data365](https://data365.co/blog/apify-twitter-scraper); [scrapegraphai](https://scrapegraphai.com/blog/apify-pricing)
- Telegram public channels can be read via the t.me/s/ web preview (Apify actors cost about $2.99–$6.80 per 1,000 results). However, the Feb 2026 Telegram ToS added an explicit scraping prohibition for "all users, businesses, and third-party services," with no research carve-out. — [Open Terms Archive](https://opentermsarchive.org/en/memos/telegram-prohibits-collecting-data-for-ai-use/); [ConductAtlas](https://conductatlas.com/platform/telegram/telegram-terms-of-service/data-scraping-prohibition-all-users/); [Apify Telegram actor](https://apify.com/renzomacar/telegram-channel-scraper)
- KOL wallet-identity datasets already exist (Solana Tracker KOL API, KOLScan, Arkham labels), so mapping X handles to wallets is possible without building it from scratch. — [Solana Tracker docs](https://docs.solanatracker.io/guides/kol-tracking)
- The sniper-cohort paper publishes a reusable detection script and a catalogue of 1,012 cohorts for pump.fun. — [arXiv 2607.02795](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.02795)

### Inferences
- A 20-KOL monitor on the official X API costs under about $100/month. That is feasible for a solo developer, and the official API avoids the ToS and legal-defensibility problems that scraping would create for an "evidence-grade" tool.
- For evidence integrity: hash each captured post (content plus metadata) and anchor batches on-chain or with RFC 3161 timestamping. Store the raw API JSON so deleted posts remain provable. (Design suggestion, not sourced.)
- Discord typically requires bot membership per server, so it is lower priority for public-evidence use. Farcaster data is open (hubs), but crypto KOL activity there is small compared with X. (Background knowledge, not verified this session.)

### Gaps
- X's own pricing page was not retrieved directly. Rates come from third-party summaries citing docs.x.com, so confirm in the Developer Console.
- X Developer Agreement terms on storing deleted posts, which affect evidence preservation, were not checked. Historically X required deleting removed content from caches, which is a legal-design issue to resolve.
- Nitter-style front ends were not checked; they are largely defunct since 2024 (background knowledge, unverified).
